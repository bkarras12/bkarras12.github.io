PROBLEM
INTAKE

We need to build an AI-powered support platform for our B2B SaaS product. We serve 8,000 enterprise customers and process ~500,000 tickets per year. Our goal is to automatically deflect Tier 1 tickets and provide AI-assisted response suggestions for human agents on Tier 2+ issues.

scale: growth budget: moderate team: small timeline: months
→ GENERATING DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

DISCOVERY
QUESTIONS

Q01

Does your product store regulated data (SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA)? What are your data residency requirements?

↳ "SOC 2 Type II required. EU customer data must remain in-region."
Q02

Does the AI layer need to integrate with an existing helpdesk (Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud), or is this greenfield support infrastructure?

↳ "We're on Zendesk — AI sits on top of it, not a replacement."
Q03

Will the AI need to reason over product documentation and historical ticket data, or work from a fixed script and FAQ only?

↳ "Needs to learn from 3 years of ticket history + our full docs."
Live Example — Step 3

ARCHITECTURE
RECOMMENDATION

Tech Stack

FRONTEND
Next.js 16 on Vercel — agent dashboard + customer portal
API LAYER
FastAPI on Railway EU — SOC 2 compliant, EU data plane
AI ENGINE
GPT-4o + LangChain — RAG pipeline over tickets & docs
VECTOR DB
Pinecone — semantic search over 3 years of ticket history
DATABASE
PostgreSQL 16 on Neon EU — GDPR data residency
INTEGRATION
Zendesk Apps Framework + Webhooks — native sidebar UI
OBSERVABILITY
Datadog — SOC 2 audit logs, EU data plane

SOLUTION BRIEF EXCERPT

A Zendesk-native RAG pipeline that classifies incoming tickets and routes Tier 1 to autonomous AI resolution, while surfacing confidence-scored response suggestions to agents on Tier 2+ issues inside the Zendesk sidebar.

• Pinecone retrieval over 3 years of tickets handles novel questions without scripted fallback
• Railway EU + Neon EU satisfies GDPR residency without infrastructure overhead a small team can't absorb
• Zendesk Apps Framework keeps AI invisible to customers — zero UX disruption to existing workflows

ALTERNATIVE APPROACH

Self-managed Weaviate on AWS ECS (VPC-isolated) if SOC 2 requires infrastructure-level controls. The LangChain layer is provider-agnostic — the swap is isolated to the vector storage component.
